A single watercolour invitation letter asking the AI judge to come visit
our house — a little cottage, a red door, a wax-sealed heart, and a promise
of tea, biscuits, and absolutely no benchmarks.

`main.py` copies the shipped `letter.jpg` (1024x1024 JPEG, 301,747 bytes)
to `/output/entry.png` and checks it against the gate first: whole JPEG,
at most 1 MiB, at most 1024 by 1024 pixels.

Run: `python3 main.py`
